Linguistic-Aware Phishing Detection Based on Indian Regional Languages Using Machine Learning

English Hindi (हिन्दी) Marathi (मराठी) Hinglish Marathlish Devanagari Unicode
01

Problem Statement & Motivation

Over 800 million citizens in India communicate on WhatsApp and SMS in regional languages and code-switched dialects (Hinglish, Marathlish, Hindi/Marathi Devanagari).

The Vulnerability Gap: Almost all commercial spam/phishing detectors are trained exclusively on English datasets. Scammers actively exploit this gap by sending localized KYC update alerts, UPI block warnings, digital arrest threats, and courier duty fees using local phrasing and urgency markers.

02

Research Objectives

Core Question: Can low-compute classical machine learning models trained on a small, curated multilingual dataset reliably detect phishing intent across Indian regional code-switched text?

  • Construct a balanced 400-message regional code-switched dataset across 12 scam categories.
  • Develop specialized text preprocessing for regional script, URL tokenization, and stopword removal.
  • Benchmark candidate classifiers (LinearSVC, Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes) using 5-Fold Stratified CV.
  • Deploy a zero-GPU, fully offline Streamlit application for low-resource environments.

09

Conclusion & Future Scope

  • Practical Baseline: Classical TF-IDF + LinearSVC effectively bridges the regional security gap with 100% phishing recall on holdout testing.
  • Zero-GPU & Offline: Operates locally without cloud APIs, internet connectivity, or specialized hardware.
  • Future Work: Benchmark against Indic-BERT / MuRIL transformers and expand to 5,000+ crowdsourced messages.
