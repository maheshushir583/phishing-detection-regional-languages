Linguistic-Aware Phishing Detection in Indian Regional & Code-Switched Dialects Using Low-Compute Machine Learning
1 Problem Statement

Over 800M Indian smartphone users communicate via WhatsApp and SMS using code-switched scripts (Hinglish, Marathlish) and native Devanagari. Mainstream commercial filters are optimized for standard English, leaving non-English native communications highly vulnerable to hyper-targeted regional threats:

  • Digital Arrest & Fake Police Notice: Fabricated legal summons.
  • Instant Loan & Utility Scams: Electricity bill cutoff intimidation.
  • UPI/KYC Phishing: Urgent banking impersonation texts.
2 Research Question & Objectives
Research Question: Can an interpretable, low-compute NLP pipeline trained on a localized multi-dialect corpus outperform deep baselines in real-time inference without GPU overhead?
  1. Synthesize & balance a 400-message regional dialect scam dataset.
  2. Implement custom normalization preserving Devanagari Unicode ranges (U+0900–U+097F) with URL/digit masking.
  3. Evaluate classical models via 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation.
  4. Deploy an offline, zero-GPU Streamlit application for low-resource environments.

9 Innovation & Edge Deployability
  • Zero-GPU Footprint: Entire inference executes on commodity smartphone/desktop CPUs in < 8 ms per message.
  • Local Privacy Preserving: Does not transmit personal communication payloads to cloud APIs or foreign LLM endpoints.
  • Production-Ready Demo: Deployed as an interactive Streamlit application with live token-attribution explainability for end-user fraud education.
