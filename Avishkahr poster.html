Linguistic-Aware Phishing Detection in Indian Regional & Code-Switched Dialects Using Low-Compute Machine Learning

Hinglish (Code-Switched) Marathlish (Code-Switched) मराठी (Devanagari) हिंदी (Devanagari) Indian English Zero-GPU Offline Edge
01 Problem Statement

Over 800 million Indians communicate primarily via WhatsApp and SMS using code-switched Romanized vernaculars (Hinglish, Marathlish) and native Devanagari scripts. Mainstream commercial email and SMS filters are almost exclusively trained on Anglo-centric corpora, leaving Indian regional-language users systematically defenseless against hyper-targeted financial cyber fraud[cite: 1]:

  • Digital Arrest Fraud: Coercive audio/video summons exploiting law enforcement intimidation[cite: 1].
  • Electricity & Utility Cutoff Scams: Immediate disconnection warnings demanding urgent app APK downloads[cite: 1].
  • UPI & KYC Account Freeze: Bank account expiration texts with lookalike phishing domains[cite: 1].
02 Objectives & Research Question
Research Question: Can a classical, interpretable, low-compute ML pipeline trained on a localized multi-dialect corpus generalize to out-of-sample real-world scams without cloud API dependencies[cite: 1]?
  1. Construct a balanced 400-message regional dialect scam dataset across 5 dialect profiles[cite: 1].
  2. Engineer custom normalization preserving Devanagari Unicode ranges (U+0900–U+097F) with URL/digit masking[cite: 1].
  3. Benchmark classical classifiers under strict 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation[cite: 1].
  4. Deploy a zero-GPU, privacy-preserving edge Streamlit application for low-resource environments[cite: 1].

  • Zero-GPU Footprint: Entire inference pipeline runs locally on standard quad-core mobile/desktop CPUs in < 6.5 ms per message[cite: 1].
